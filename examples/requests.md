# Example requests

These requests illustrate different purposes and styles. They are not a fixed menu or a record of completed model evaluations. Use actual supplied sources and an available authoring backend.

## Management decision

User: “把这些项目资料做成 10 页的决策汇报，听众是管理层，要清楚说明依据、备选方案和需要决定的事项，沿用附件品牌。”

Expected behavior: build a complete deck around the decision; preserve evidence and uncertainty, respect the total page count and supplied brand, and avoid inventing results.

## Teaching

User: “根据附件教材，给高中生做光合作用课件，要有示意图、例题和课堂练习，不要套产品推销结构。”

Expected behavior: sequence concepts by prerequisites; use accurate explanations and helpful visual examples; retain correct answers in suitable notes or follow-up pages, within the requested length.

## Research defense

User: “根据论文和实验图表做 15 分钟的答辩，听众懂这个专业，保留方法、实验条件、局限和引用。”

Expected behavior: preserve the actual research argument and qualifications, make figures readable, and use the duration as a pacing constraint without an automatic slide-count formula.

## Product and sales

User: “给采购团队介绍这个设备，重点说明适用场景、操作步骤和维护要求，以提供的手册为准。”

Expected behavior: organize around the buyer's real questions; use sourced specifications and actual images; keep proposed performance claims distinct from documented capability.

## Data review

User: “用这份月度经营数据做可独立阅读的分析，保留单位、统计口径和异常说明，图表和表格可编辑。”

Expected behavior: choose quantitative representations for the analytical questions, preserve qualifications, retain reading context and produce editable evidence.

## Portfolio

User: “把我的作品和项目说明做成设计作品集，编辑设计风格，突出实际贡献，保留原始作品比例。”

Expected behavior: let the actual work drive a varied visual sequence, clarify the person's contribution, and avoid imposing a corporate or technical outline.

## Cultural or personal story

User: “用这些照片和采访文字做一份社区记忆分享，摄影为主、暖色、少字，配适量讲述备注。”

Expected behavior: create a coherent visual story, preserve real names/dates and authorized assets, and keep speaker explanation separate from the visible page.

## Mixed or unlisted purpose

User: “我要在博物馆向亲子家庭介绍一件科学展品，既要准确，也要有探索感，最后安排一个问题。”

Expected behavior: combine public education, explanation and storytelling; accept the request without forcing it into a predefined category or prescribing robot icons or a technical palette.

## Style independent of topic

User: “做一份黑白摄影风的年度业务回顾，品牌只用这两种颜色，数据以附件为准。”

Expected behavior: honor the requested visual identity while keeping figures readable, evidence accurate and charts editable. The topic alone does not determine the style.

## Local edit and audit

User: “只调整第 4 页的图片和正文位置，其余页面和全部文字不变。”

Expected behavior: perform the scoped edit without running a full storytelling redesign.

User: “只检查这份 PPT 的前 6 页，不修改，说明哪些问题已确认、哪些需要打开文件复核。”

Expected behavior: report located findings, preserve the input and distinguish structural evidence from actual rendering.
